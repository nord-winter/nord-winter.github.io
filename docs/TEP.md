# Target Employer Profile
**Vladislav Simutin · v1.0 · May 2026**

---

## 1. Company Profile

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Stage** | Series B / Series C |
| **Size** | 50 – 500 employees |
| **Model** | Product company (not agency, not outsource) |
| **Work format** | Remote-first (not "remote-friendly") |
| **Geography** | EU · US · APAC |
| **Funding** | $30M+ raised, active growth phase |

---

## 2. Target Niches

### Priority 1 — Fintech / Crypto
- High reliability demands align with InfoSec background
- Compliance and data security are first-class concerns — real differentiator
- Compensation ceiling is highest in this segment
- Examples of company types: payment infrastructure, lending platforms, crypto custody, trading systems

### Priority 1 — DevTools / Infra Products
- Go-heavy by culture and stack
- Engineers who built real systems are valued over credential collectors
- Interesting technical problems: reliability, observability, distributed systems
- Examples of company types: developer platforms, internal tooling products, cloud-native infra

### Priority 2 — E-commerce / Marketplace
- ASCENDER experience translates directly — easier narrative
- Lower compensation ceiling than Fintech/DevTools
- Consider if Fintech/DevTools pipeline is slow

---

## 3. Stack Requirements (Must-Have)

The target company must use Go as a core backend language — not as tooling or scripting, but as the primary service layer.

**Green flags in job descriptions:**
- Go / Golang in requirements (not "nice to have")
- Kubernetes, Terraform, GCP/AWS in infrastructure
- Mentions of observability: Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry
- Microservices or distributed systems architecture
- Words: reliability, scalability, platform, infrastructure

**Red flags:**
- "Go is a plus" — PHP/Python/Ruby is core, Go is secondary
- "We are moving to microservices" — greenfield chaos without real load
- No mention of testing or observability culture
- Job description is a generic template with no technical specifics

## 4. Culture Requirements

| Signal | How to verify |
|--------|---------------|
| Remote-first | Glassdoor reviews, async communication culture mentioned in JD |
| Engineering-driven | Tech blog exists, engineers speak at conferences |
| No FAANG-only filter | JD focuses on problems solved, not logos on resume |
| Direct hiring (no recruiter black hole) | Wellfound / LinkedIn — founder or eng manager posts job |
| Transparent compensation | Salary range visible in JD or Levels.fyi data available |

---

## 5. Anti-Profile — Do Not Apply

| Signal | Reason |
|--------|--------|
| "Office required" or "hybrid 3+ days" | Incompatible with Bangkok location |
| Enterprise, 5000+ employees | FAANG logo filter, slow process, low eng impact |
| Agency / outsource / body-shop | Wrong environment for platform engineering depth |
| "CTO or senior engineer" — vague scope | Bait-and-switch into management |
| No Go in core stack | Wrong technical fit |
| Seed stage, no funding confirmed | Salary risk, instability |

## 7. Application Rules

- **10 targeted applications per week** — quality over volume
- **Filter before applying:** Go in core stack + remote-first + Series B/C
- **Personalize opening line** — one sentence connecting their specific problem to your experience
- **No cover letter unless required** — resume + tailored first message is enough
- **Track every application** — date, channel, stage, feedback (see job-search-tracker)

---

## 8. Evaluation Checklist

Before applying, confirm:

- [ ] Go is in core backend stack (not "nice to have")
- [ ] Remote-first confirmed (not just "allowed")
- [ ] Series B/C stage (funded, not pre-revenue)
- [ ] Fintech or DevTools/Infra niche (or strong ASCENDER relevance for e-commerce)
- [ ] No FAANG-only filter visible in JD tone
- [ ] Salary range confirmed or estimable via Levels.fyi / Glassdoor
